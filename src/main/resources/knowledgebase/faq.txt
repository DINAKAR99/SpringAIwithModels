MIV Sand Booking – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is MIV?
MIV (Mana Integrated Vehicle / Sand Management System) is the official online platform used for sand booking, allocation, tracking, and delivery management within Telangana.

2. How do I book sand through MIV?
To book sand, visit the official MIV website, create or log in to your account, enter your location and required quantity, verify your details, and complete the payment process.

3. How can I track the status of my sand booking?
You can track your booking status by entering your Booking ID or Registered Mobile Number in the “Track Order / Booking Status” section on the MIV website.

4. What documents or details are required for sand booking?
You may be required to provide:
• Valid Mobile Number
• Delivery Address Details
• Purpose of Sand Usage (e.g., Personal Construction, Commercial Use)
Additional documents may apply depending on local regulations.

5. Can I cancel my sand booking?
Cancellation depends on the order status. If your booking has not been approved or dispatched, cancellation may be possible. Once transportation has started, cancellation may not be permitted.

6. What should I do if payment is deducted but the booking is not confirmed?
In most cases, the deducted amount will be automatically refunded by the payment gateway within 3–7 business days. If the issue persists, contact MIV support with the transaction ID.

7. What is the estimated delivery time for sand?
Delivery timelines vary based on location, stock availability, and transportation conditions. Typically, delivery can take between 2 to 7 working days.

8. How can I contact customer support?
You can contact MIV Customer Support via the helpline number displayed on the official website, or by using the support/helpdesk section for grievances and queries.

9. Can I change the delivery address after booking?
Address modification requests may be allowed only before dispatch. Once transportation is confirmed, changes may not be accepted.

10. Is there any restriction on the quantity of sand that can be booked?
Yes. Quantity limits may vary based on purpose (personal / commercial) and regulatory guidelines. Please refer to the booking guidelines section on the MIV portal.
